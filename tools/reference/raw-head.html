My Most Mostest

A children's picture book - written by Brie Hollingsworth Krebs, illustrated by Elle Harting. A book of favorites.

My Most Mostest is a joyful rhyming picture book that celebrates all of a child's very favorite things - from soaring on the highest swing to snuggling up with a lovey, and getting gloriously messy and muddy along the way.
